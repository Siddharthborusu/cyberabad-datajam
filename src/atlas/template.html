Methodology

This atlas visualises Hyderabad's scheduled public transport network and spatial connectivity. It is built from:

  • HMRL Metro GTFS (September 2026) — route geometry from shapes.txt
  • TGSRTC bus GTFS (June 2026) — 4,709 stops, scheduled service counts
  • MMTS commuter rail GTFS — station sequences derived as LineStrings
  • OSM drive network — major road classes for geographic context
  • Processed multimodal hub analysis — spatial proximity + bus catchment

Important: All service figures represent scheduled trips, not live operational frequency or reliability. Distance values are straight-line distances unless stated otherwise — not pedestrian routes.

Potential multimodal hubs indicate spatial proximity and scheduled bus-service context. They do not establish interchange quality or pedestrian accessibility.
